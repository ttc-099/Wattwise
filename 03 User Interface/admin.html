    WattWise suggests the split · the scheme admin approves it · SDP files with the Energy Commission · 120-day notice per buyer (Energy Commission, 2025). The tool never sends on its own.

    Settlements

    A month settles after it has happened. TNB reads every buyer's real meter and each allocation is checked against what the buyer actually used. Solar allocated beyond that use is given away; the held-back slice is sold via NEDA at 8 sen/kWh. One modelled year at Elmina’s size: the load shapes come from two real buildings, scaled to the scheme’s assumed use (Miller et al., 2020; WattWise, 2026).
